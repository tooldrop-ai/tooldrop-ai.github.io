The best AI writing tools

AI writing tools split into editors that improve what you wrote and generators that draft new text. Most people get the most value from a good editor plus a general assistant like ChatGPT or Claude.

How to choose

  • Try the browser extension in the apps you actually use.
  • Check privacy settings for confidential documents.

FAQ

What is the best free AI writing tool?

Grammarly, QuillBot, DeepL and Wordtune all have free tiers. General assistants like ChatGPT and Claude are also free to start and strong at drafting.

Is Grammarly or QuillBot better?

Grammarly is better as an everyday editor for grammar and clarity; QuillBot is better at paraphrasing and summarizing.

Can Google tell if content is AI-written?

Google says it rewards helpful, original content regardless of how it's produced, and penalizes low-quality content made mainly to rank. Edit and add your own expertise.
