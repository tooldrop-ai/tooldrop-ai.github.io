The best AI meeting note takers

AI note takers record or listen to your meetings, then produce transcripts, summaries and action items. The main differences are how they join calls, which platforms they support, and how they handle privacy.

How to choose

  • Bot that joins the call, or on-device recording?
  • Check support for your platform (Zoom, Meet, Teams).
  • Always tell participants and follow local recording-consent rules (in the EU, GDPR applies).
  • Check where recordings are stored and for how long.

FAQ

What is the best AI note taker for meetings?

Otter.ai and Fireflies.ai are the most established; Krisp is a good option if you'd rather not have a bot join your calls.

Is it legal to record meetings with AI?

Rules vary by country. In the EU, you generally need to inform participants and have a lawful basis under GDPR. When in doubt, ask for consent.

Are there free AI meeting note takers?

Otter.ai, Fireflies.ai and Krisp offer free tiers with monthly limits.
