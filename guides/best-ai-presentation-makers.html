Gamma

Best for: Fastest prompt-to-deck

FreemiumProductivity

Gamma generates complete, well-designed presentations, documents and simple web pages from a prompt or pasted notes, and makes restyling a whole deck quick.

Keep in mind: Its card-based format differs from classic slides; check export to PowerPoint fits your needs.

Google Gemini

How to choose

  • Paste the same outline into two tools and compare the first draft.
  • Check export formats (PPTX, PDF) if you present from other software.
  • Keep facts and figures your own: AI drafts can contain errors.
  • For brand decks, prioritize template and font control.

FAQ

What is the best AI tool for making presentations?

Gamma is one of the fastest ways to go from a prompt to a finished-looking deck. Canva is better when you need detailed design control, and Copilot or Gemini make sense if you live in PowerPoint or Google Slides.

Is there a free AI presentation maker?

Gamma and Canva both offer free plans with limits on AI usage or premium features.

Can AI make a PowerPoint from a document?

Yes. Copilot in PowerPoint and Gamma can both build a deck from an existing document or notes, which you then edit.
