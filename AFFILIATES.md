## Step by step

1. **Before you start:** a PayPal account (or bank details) in your name, and your Belgian address. US programs will ask for a **W-8BEN** tax form (non-US individual; you can claim the Belgium–US treaty). It's a form, not a cost.
2. Sign up at **https://partnerstack.com** (partner/affiliate account), then open each program's link above and apply.
3. Apply to Krisp and Synthesia via their pages.
4. When approved, copy your affiliate link and run, for example:
   `python3 scripts/set_affiliate.py elevenlabs "https://try.elevenlabs.io/yourcode"`
   then `git add -A && git commit -m "Affiliate links" && git push`.
   (Or paste the links into `affiliates.json` and run `python3 scripts/build.py`.) Visit buttons switch to the affiliate link with `rel="sponsored"`; the disclosure is already on every page.
5. Keep at it: Looka removes affiliates with no customer in 3 months, and most programs drop inactive accounts after about a year.

## Belgium/EU notes (not legal or tax advice)
- Commissions are taxable income in Belgium. Occasional small amounts may count as "miscellaneous income"; regular activity may need self-employed (side-activity) status, and above certain thresholds VAT registration. A short check with an accountant or your local business counter (ondernemingsloket / guichet d'entreprises) is worth it once money comes in.
- Disclosure: the EU consumer-protection rules (Unfair Commercial Practices Directive) and the FTC (for US visitors) require clear disclosure. ToolDrop shows it in the footer, on affiliate tool pages and at `/disclosure.html`.
- ToolDrop sets no cookies or analytics itself; affiliate cookies are set by the merchant after the click, under their own policy.
